Help to teach your children about different fruits with this fun wooden fruit set. Great for role-playing fun your children can play at being a chef and they can learn how to count as they count the individual pieces when they're whole and when they're cut up.

The set includes a solid wooden knife and ten pieces of solid wood fruit that can all be cut into two pieces. When the fruit is cut it makes a fun crunching sound to add a more realistic level to their play.

Size H17, W19.5, D6cm.
For ages 2 years and over.

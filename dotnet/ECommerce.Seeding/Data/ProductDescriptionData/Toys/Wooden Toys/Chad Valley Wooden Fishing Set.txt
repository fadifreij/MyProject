Lets go fishing. This colourful fishing set is wonderful for improving your children's hand-eye co-ordination as they use the magnetic fishing rods to pick up different sea creatures. With two rods your children can play with their friends as they both improve their fine motor skills, as the fish have a small magnet attached to them that needs a precise touch. Your little ones will love diving into this game and developing their problem solving skills as they learn to work things out for themselves.

Made from wood, this colourful set is built to last. It can be used as a game to see who can pick up the most underwater animals or as a learning tool as children catch and then name each character.

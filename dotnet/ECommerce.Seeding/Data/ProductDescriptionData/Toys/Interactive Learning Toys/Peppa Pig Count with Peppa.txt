Count with Peppa' helps develop counting skills, colour and picture recognition. With 10 interactive play coins to put into Peppa's purse, there are 8 different activities - Peppa recognises which coins have been inserted and responds accordingly with one of the activity games.

Also plays 3 songs from the TV series.

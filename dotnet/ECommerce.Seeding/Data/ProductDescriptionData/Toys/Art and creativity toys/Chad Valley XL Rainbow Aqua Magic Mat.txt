This extra large aqua magic playmat is perfect for getting your little ones into creative play. They will love expressing themselves creatively as they draw anything that pops into their imagination. Easy to use, just fill the pens with water and watch your children go, as pictures appear in rainbow bright colours. Being extra large your children can invite a friend over to draw with them.

This set is great for mess-free play and encourages your children to improve their fine motor skills as they hold the pen to draw.

Once your children have finished with their creations, wait a few minutes while it dries and the images will disappear so they can start all over again.

Kids can enjoy wildlife adventures with this LEGO Creator 3in1 Wild Animals: Panda Family playset then rebuild it into an orca or penguin family.

LEGO® model number: 31165 - for ages 8 years and over.

Theme: LEGO Creator.

3 animal playsets in 1 box – The LEGO Creator Wild Animals: Panda Family set lets kids build 3 different animal families with the same bricks.
Endless play – Kids can choose to build a panda toy, an orca toy or a penguin figure, each with its offspring (models cannot be built simultaneously).
Posable animal figures – The panda's mouth, head and paws can move, the orca can move its body and fin and the penguin's flippers and feet can move.
For play and display – When playtime is over, the LEGO animal figures look great on display, with 2 transparent stands provided for the orca family.
A fun way to build – Let the LEGO Builder app allows kids to save sets, track progress and zoom in and rotate their toy animals in 3D while they build.
The panda toy stands over 14 cm (5.5 in. ) tall.

The Fisher-Price Simply Senses Newborn Gym is a colourful baby activity mat that grows with your little one from lay & play and tummy time to travel. The gym features an arch with 6 repositionable sensory toys including a portable plush koala with a soft chime and a take-along mobile that can be attached to a pushchair for fun on-the-go. Other sensory toys include a self-discovery mirror, goose rattle, BPA-free moth teether and soft crinkle toy that can be repositioned on the gym arch or used for travel play.

Experience crystal clear picture and sound with this striking, near bezel-less UHD 4K TV from Samsung – the World's best-selling TV brand. The DU7100 is new for 2024, and can display over 1 million shades of colour in glorious 4K HDR powered by HDR10+. There is Adaptive Sound too, so it sounds as good as it looks whatever you are watching. This Smart TV is powered by Tizen, so you can access all your favourite digital TV channels, a huge collection of 4K films, and your catch-up TV apps seamlessly through the Samsung Universal Guide.

Samsung TV Plus provides instant access to loads of extra free TV channels, straight out of the box – just connect to the internet for movies, entertainment, sports, news, and children's channels. No need for sign-ups, subscriptions or credit cards – it's 100% free.

Samsung Crystal Processor 4K will upscale your favourite content to 4K Ultra HD. Whether you are watching YouTube videos or streaming the latest boxset, your Samsung TV will work in the background to upgrade your picture and sound quality.

Samsung Gaming Hub brings together the biggest console and streaming games from Xbox and other industry-leading partners, all in on one place. There are no downloads, storage limits, or console required - just turn on your TV and play. Game Pass and compatible controller required.

For a truly immersive viewing experience, you need great sound. This Samsung TV has Object Tracking Sound Lite, Adaptive Sound technology, and if you add a Samsung Q-Symphony sound bar (sold separately) both the TV and sound bar speakers play together in perfect harmony.

Access all your favourite Smart TV apps - including Netflix, BBC iPlayer, Disney+, Apple TV, NOW, and many more! Some apps may not be available at launch.

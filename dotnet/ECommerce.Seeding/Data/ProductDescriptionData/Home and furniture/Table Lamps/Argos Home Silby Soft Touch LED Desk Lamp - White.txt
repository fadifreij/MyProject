Perfect for reading, working and tinkering away, the clever Silby soft touch LED desk lamp has a flexible neck and head so you can direct the light to where you need it most. The touch sensor on-off switch on the base of the lamp offers 3 levels of brightness for the ultimate in task light convenience. And the tactile matte finish of the silicone adds a modern touch.

Comes with a pre-installed LED bulb so all you need to do is simply plug it into the wall (this one's mains operated) and hey presto! No additional bulbs required.
